This tool is provided for informational purposes only and does not constitute formal tax or legal advice. It is not a substitute for professional advice on your own circumstances, and using it creates no professional relationship of any kind.

The index is assembled automatically from fund company websites and may be incomplete, out of date, or wrong. Some managers are not yet included, some are listed with statements that must be retrieved by hand, and older tax years are not indexed.

Where a manager is shown with no statement available, that records only what was found on their public website on the date given. It is not a statement about whether that manager will issue one — one may be available on request, so ask them directly.

Privacy. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and stores nothing about you or your searches. Documents you drop in are read inside your own browser and are never uploaded — there is no server to upload them to.
